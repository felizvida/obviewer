# Product

## Who it's for

The maintainer and a few colleagues who keep notes in Markdown (Obsidian vaults, plain folders, GitHub READMEs) or in a DokuWiki, and who want to read them comfortably without any risk of changing them.

## Promise

- **Open anything, read it properly.** Double-click a `.md` file, open a folder, a vault or a wiki, and see it rendered faithfully: diagrams, math, callouts, embeds, tables.
- **Never change a note.** Read-only access enforced by macOS. Editing happens in your own editor, with Sublime Text by default, and Obviewer refreshes when you save.
- **Share in the format people use.** Rich text for Mail and Word, a Slack-friendly copy, print, and a PDF via Preview.
- **Stay light and offline.** No accounts, no sync, no network use, no plugins.

## In scope

- Reading single files, Markdown folders, Obsidian vaults and DokuWiki folders
- Search, wiki-link navigation, backlinks, outline, find, back/forward, and a note graph
- Finder integration: the default `.md` app, Quick Look, note windows, Open Recent
- An "Edit in…" handoff with live refresh, and sharing that needs no write access

## Out of scope

- Editing inside Obviewer, or any feature that needs write access to notes
- Running Obsidian plugins, such as Dataview queries
- Sync, collaboration and cloud services
- Mac App Store distribution, for now

## Quality bar

- Rendering matches what the note's own app shows, for common syntax
- Notes open within moments, and the reader never shows a blank page without explaining why
- Every limitation is written down in [`STATUS.md`](./STATUS.md)
